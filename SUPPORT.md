# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Start with the [documentation](docs/README.md) and [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md). `mu doctor` checks the installation, the judges and the connections, and tells you what to fix.
- **Questions and ideas:** [Discussions](https://github.com/qybaihe/mu/discussions). Search first; someone may have asked already.
- **A bug:** [open an issue](https://github.com/qybaihe/mu/issues/new/choose) with the bug report form. Include the version, the platform and the steps to reproduce.
- **A security problem:** report it privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Never in a public issue.

English and Chinese are both fine everywhere. 中文提问、报 bug 都可以。

mu is maintained by a small team in their spare time. Issues with a clear reproduction get answered first.
